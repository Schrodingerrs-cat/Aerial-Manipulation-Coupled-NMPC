Frozen before the run: `Code/config/am3_freeze_v1.json` (sha256 of 22 files, verified unchanged afterwards), acceptance `Code/config/am3_acceptance_v1.json` (copy of the draft written before any held-out run; thresholds derived from the task, not from results: base 20 mm, pad tracking RMS 3 mm, endpoint 5 mm, coverage 0.95 within 5 mm, contact-valid 0.95, peak Fn 4 N, <= 2 losses, rotor reserve 0.3 N, tilt 15 deg, no unexpected contact, no solver fallback). Caveats. (1) A pass is functional only; timing is separate: batch timing was measured with 8 concurrent processes and is not valid (N median 17.8 ms, 21 % of cycles above 20 ms). (2) N was tuned on development scenarios and one validation observation (x-weight of the pad residual doubled after one contact loss in validation, `results/am3_runs/val_N_before_xweight`); held-out data were not used. (3) B received a larger tuning budget than N (`Findings/Codebase/controllers_v3.md`) and runs at 200 Hz; the B/N difference includes rate, architecture and model. Passing thresholds are task-derived; B's raw error distribution is reported so other thresholds can be judged. (4) The nominal predictor matches the plant to integration/contact calibration, so N's accuracy is an upper bound for mismatch conditions. (5) Nd: nine softer weight sets on four dev scenarios (`results/am3_nd_tuning/`) survived 3-8 s at best and none completed; a stable decoupled NMPC may exist outside that search. The solver hang appears only in diverging Nd runs; the failsafe (tilt > 40 deg or base error > 250 mm, identical for all controllers) was added after it.

## Timing and deadline trials (quiet machine, one process at a time; `Code/scripts/am3_timing.py`, `Code/results/am3_timing/summary.json`, `Code/results/am3_runs/timing/`)
N, single-thread acados SQP-RTI, N = 10, h = 20 ms, nsub 4: full-cycle time (reference preview + delay-compensating propagation + solve) median 15.0-15.5 ms, p95 16-20 ms, p99 20-35 ms, max 40-109 ms (the 109 ms is the first call); 1.3-1.7 % of cycles exceed the 20 ms period, in one burst of about 12 consecutive cycles at 39 ms while the pad closes the last centimetres to the wall (approach phase) plus the first call. **N is therefore not real-time compliant as built.** B takes about 1.2 ms against its 5 ms period (max 3-9 ms).
* Deadline ENFORCED (a result later than the period is discarded and the previous command stays): N lost control (failsafe) at about 5.4 s on all three paths: the 39 ms burst becomes about 20 consecutive discarded commands during approach, the estimate degrades, and the problem gets harder. This is a worst-case policy (late results discarded rather than applied late).
* Injected random misses (command held): N completed all three paths with 2 % and 10 % misses (base max <= 1.4 mm). B completed 2 % on line and rounded triangle (base max 42 and 29 mm), lost control at 2 % on the circle and at 10 % on all three.
Possible remedies not yet tried (would need a new candidate and freeze v2): bounded QP iterations, fewer substeps/horizon stages, OpenMP build, constraint scheduling near contact.
