## E. Implications that bear on decisions now
1. The two closest underactuated references (R3 for contact; S7/S11 for geometry) show underactuated-vehicle contact is achievable with a rigid 1-DoF arm and classical compensation; our pitch-joint decision therefore stays conditional (see `math_spec.md` section 5).
2. R3 reports friction as a major disturbance during contact; our plant confirms friction can break the dwell (`contact_model_findings.md` item 7). Friction should be an explicit mismatch dimension.
3. S12 indicates an observer without a separate contact-force measurement cannot split contact from disturbance; the fairness rule (same measurements for all methods) must state whether the contact normal force is measured. Current simple controller assumes it is.
4. R1, R2, S1, S6, S10, S12 use fully actuated platforms: results do not transfer unchanged.
